Privacy Policy — Army Readiness

Last updated: August 2026

Army Readiness ("the app") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data with the developer or any third party.

Data Storage

All information you enter into the app — including name, rank, ASVAB scores, physical fitness results, VA compensation figures, and any other input — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to a server, and is never accessible to the developer.

In-App Purchases

The app offers optional tip jar purchases, processed entirely through Apple's App Store and StoreKit (iOS) or Google Play and Google Play Billing (Android), depending on which version of the app you're using. The developer does not receive, store, or have access to your payment information, account ID, or purchase history. These transactions are governed by the relevant platform's own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy or policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytics and Advertising

This app contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks of any kind.

PDF Exports

Documents generated by the app (score summaries, official form fill-ins) are created locally on your device and only shared if you explicitly choose to do so via your device's native share function (e.g., saving to Files, printing, or emailing).

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.

Changes to This Policy

Any future changes to this privacy policy will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
