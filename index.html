Frequently asked

Is this an official Army app?
No. Army Digital Leaders Book is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Army or Department of War. All standards, forms, and figures are unofficial references — always verify against official Army sources before relying on them.

Does the app work offline?
Yes. All the app functionality work fully offline. An internet connection is only used for the optional Tip Jar purchases.

Where does my data go?
Nowhere. Everything you enter stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
